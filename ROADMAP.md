# Tilden and aibill: product direction

**Building financial infrastructure for the AI workforce.** AI spend review is
the starting point. Connecting cost, responsibility and useful outcomes is the
longer-term ambition. Direction is not a delivery guarantee.

Updated October 6, 2026. Published CLI changes are recorded in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); [source coverage](https://asktilden.com/docs/sources)
describes availability by surface.

## Free local tools available now

- **aibill CLI:** local Claude Code/Codex usage and API-rate estimates, optional
  provider cost reports, source/coverage labels and saved reports. No account is
  needed for the local workflow.
- **Local investigation:** Context Health, optional cache-only Claude Code
  statusline, inspection plans and guided token experiments. Experiments need
  matched completed sessions and user-declared quality; they do not prove cash
  savings, accepted business outcomes or ROI.
- **Local MCP and Codex plugin:** selected evidence on demand in a compatible
  AI client. This is separate from hosted Workspace MCP.
- **Explicit Workspace machine commands:** pairing and consented uploads of
  eligible Claude Code/Codex session facts for the invited Workspace owner. The
  pilot has one owner seat. This is not a complete ledger upload, background sync
  or provider-billing connection.
- **Developer packages:** shared evidence contracts, source readers and report
  renderers; see the [supported library preview](docs/LIBRARY.md).

OpenAI and Anthropic CLI connectors have non-empty live verification. Cursor
and Copilot remain fixture-verified beta pending real-account acceptance.
Gemini CLI is experimental and fixture-verified, limited to financial
CLI/report/local MCP surfaces; it does not feed Context Health, statusline,
Glance, Apply or plan/runway. Glance is a source-built macOS preview with no
signed public download yet.

## Workspace and its connected experiences

**Workspace is invitation-only, with assisted onboarding.** It supports a shared
review of supported provider costs, projects/models, briefings, coverage and
budget settings alongside explicitly shared machine activity. A configured
budget is not a provider spending limit. Missing days remain missing, and
provider subtotals are not final invoices.

**Workspace MCP** provides assisted, read-only access to hosted reports in
compatible AI clients. **Plugin / ChatGPT Pilot** uses that hosted connection;
it is private and not publicly listed. Availability must be confirmed during
onboarding. Neither is installed by the local Codex plugin.

OpenAI and Anthropic are the core hosted cost sources. Cursor and Copilot
require separate guided pilot setup; live partner validation remains pending.
Additional providers are considered in response to partner demand, with no
connector or delivery commitment. A listed model price does not imply a
provider integration or complete historical coverage.

[Join the waitlist](https://asktilden.com/?ref=github-roadmap#beta) for updates and
access invitations. A signup does not create a Workspace or enable a source.

## What needs more evidence

- Complete real-account acceptance and recovery checks for guided beta sources
  before broadening availability claims.
- Improve source coverage and explain unavailable history without turning
  unknown records into zero spend.
- Make first spending reviews easier for engineering and finance to reproduce
  across the same dates and accounts.
- Validate private AI-client access and lifecycle behavior before making public
  Plugin availability claims.
- Explore links between cost, responsibility and outcomes with partner evidence.
  Defensible ROI also needs independently measured monetary value and a basis
  for comparison; activity or token volume alone cannot supply it.
- Validate signed, notarized Glance distribution before offering a public Mac
  download.

## Explicit limits

There is no claim of autonomous provider changes, automatic optimization,
automated business-outcome measurement, enterprise certifications, full-history
coverage or general availability of every connector. The free CLI remains
useful without Workspace. The product is not a general trace explorer, prompt
warehouse or model gateway.

[Request a source or propose an improvement](https://github.com/futurastudio/ai-spend-agent/issues/new/choose)
with the user decision and the evidence it requires. Keep real credentials and
private transcripts out of issues.
